# Renting a home in Dubai

## What a tenant pays
- Annual rent, usually paid in 1 to 4 cheques. Fewer cheques can help the tenant negotiate a better price.
- Security deposit: 5% of the annual rent for an unfurnished unit, 10% for a furnished unit.
- Agency fee: 5% of the annual rent plus 5% VAT.
- Ejari registration: about AED 220.
- DEWA (water and electricity) deposit: AED 2,000 for an apartment, AED 4,000 for a villa.

## Documents needed
Passport, UAE residence visa and Emirates ID. Company leases also need a trade licence.

## How long it takes
Once the tenant chooses a unit and pays, the contract and Ejari are usually done in 2 to 3 working days.

## Viewings for rentals
Tenants can book a viewing with the AI assistant. For rentals we only arrange viewings for tenants who can move in within 3 months.
