# Buying a property in Dubai: steps and fees

Figures are typical for 2026 and shown for illustration. The client should confirm exact amounts with a consultant before signing.

## Who can buy
Foreigners can buy freehold property in designated areas, which include Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, JVC, JLT, Dubai Hills Estate, Arabian Ranches, Dubai Creek Harbour and Palm Jumeirah. You do not need to live in the UAE to buy.

## Steps for a ready (secondary) property
1. Viewing and offer. We arrange viewings and send your offer to the seller.
2. Memorandum of Understanding (Form F). Buyer and seller sign it, and the buyer gives a 10% security cheque.
3. No Objection Certificate (NOC). The seller gets it from the developer. It usually takes 3 to 10 working days.
4. Transfer. Everyone meets at a Dubai Land Department trustee office. The title deed is issued in the buyer's name the same day.
A cash purchase usually takes 3 to 5 weeks. With a mortgage, allow 6 to 8 weeks.

## Buying costs (on top of the price)
- Dubai Land Department (DLD) transfer fee: 4% of the price, plus a small admin fee.
- Title deed and trustee office fees: about AED 4,000 to 6,000 in total.
- Agency commission: 2% of the price plus 5% VAT.
- Mortgage registration (only with a mortgage): 0.25% of the loan amount plus an admin fee.
As a rule of thumb, budget about 7% on top of the price for a cash purchase.

## Off-plan purchases
For off-plan units you pay the developer directly on a payment plan. The DLD fee of 4% is usually paid at booking. There is no agency commission charged to the buyer on our off-plan launches.
