# Mortgages and payment plans

## Mortgages for residents and non-residents
- UAE residents (expats) can usually borrow up to 80% of the value for a first home up to AED 5 million, and up to 70% above that.
- UAE nationals can usually borrow up to 85% for a first home.
- Non-residents can get a mortgage from some banks, usually up to 50% to 60% of the value.
- Typical terms: up to 25 years, the loan must end before age 65 (salaried) or 70 (self-employed).
- A mortgage pre-approval takes about 3 to 7 working days and shows the seller you are a serious buyer.
