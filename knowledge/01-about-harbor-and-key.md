# About Harbor & Key Realty (demo agency)

Harbor & Key Realty is a **fictional** Dubai real estate agency used to demo this AI sales agent. All names, listings and prices in this folder are sample data.

## Office and hours
- Office: Level 12, Marina Plaza, Dubai Marina, Dubai, UAE
- Working days: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 (Dubai time). Closed on Sunday.
- Property viewings run Monday to Saturday between 10:00 and 18:00. Each viewing slot is 1 hour.
- Languages spoken by the team: English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi and Russian.

## Our team
- Sara Malik, Senior Sales Consultant: Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah.
- Omar Haddad, Sales and Leasing Consultant: JVC, JLT, studios and investor units. Speaks Arabic.
- Daniel Reyes, Sales Consultant: Business Bay, Dubai Hills Estate villas.
- Layla Nasser, Off-plan Specialist: Dubai Creek Harbour, Arabian Ranches, new launches. Speaks Arabic.

## What we do
- Sales of ready and off-plan apartments, townhouses and villas.
- Annual rentals (we do not offer holiday homes or short stays).
- Help with mortgages through partner banks and brokers.
- After-sales help: transfer at the Dubai Land Department trustee office, Ejari, DEWA and tenancy setup.

## Contact rules for the AI assistant
- The AI assistant answers questions, qualifies buyers and tenants, and books viewings.
- It never promises a discount, a price change, or a guaranteed rental yield. Price negotiation is always handled by a human consultant.
- Legal, tax and visa questions get general information only, and the client is offered a call with a consultant.
