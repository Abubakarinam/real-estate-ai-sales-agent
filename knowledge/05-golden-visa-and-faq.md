# Golden Visa and frequent questions

## Golden Visa through property
A buyer can apply for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa when the property value is AED 2 million or more. This can be one property or several properties that together reach AED 2 million. Mortgaged and some off-plan properties can qualify under conditions. The visa can include the spouse and children. We give general guidance only and refer clients to an approved typing centre or the official channels for the application.

## Frequent questions
**Can I buy without visiting Dubai?** Yes. We can do video viewings, and the transfer can be done with a power of attorney.

**Is the price negotiable?** Many sellers accept offers. A consultant handles every negotiation. The AI assistant cannot agree a price.

**Do you charge a fee to show me a property?** No. Viewings are free.

**Can I see a property today?** Viewings need at least a few hours' notice. The assistant can show the next free slots.

**Do you do short-term or holiday rentals?** No, only annual rentals and sales.

**Which areas are best for families?** Dubai Hills Estate and Arabian Ranches are popular for schools, parks and villas.

**Which areas are best for investors?** JVC studios and 1-beds for yield. Downtown Dubai and Palm Jumeirah for capital value.
